Questions

How does it work?
CorrectMe runs as a menu bar app. Select any text in any app, press the hotkey (default ⌘⇧E), and your selected text is sent to the AI provider of your choice. The corrected text replaces the original instantly.
Do I need an AI API key?
Yes — CorrectMe connects to your own AI account (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, or OpenRouter). This keeps your data private and your costs predictable. For most users, API costs are well under $1/month. OpenRouter has models with a free tier.
What macOS version is required?
macOS 13 Ventura or later.
Can I use it in any language?
Yes. CorrectMe detects the language of your text automatically and corrects it in that language. Vietnamese (tiếng Việt) is specifically supported, including diacritics and tone marks.
What happens after I buy?
You'll receive a license key by email within a minute of purchase. Enter it in CorrectMe → Activate License… to unlock the app.
Can I get a refund?
Yes — 30-day no-questions-asked refund. Just reply to your purchase email.
